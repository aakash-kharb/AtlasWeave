# Brand direction

The user-supplied `LOGO.png` combines an Atlas-like supporting A with an interwoven globe. It expresses both the name and connected data without a realistic figure. The browser uses an unchanged packaged copy of this source image and a navy/ivory palette. The small mark is framed with CSS; the image pixels are not edited.

Suggested refinements for a future logo pass:

- Keep the A and woven globe, but remove one or two of the narrow ribbon fragments. At favicon sizes, they merge into noise.
- Make the negative-space gaps and ribbon widths more consistent, especially at the globe's lower edge.
- Tighten the artboard around the emblem so the mark occupies more of its canvas.
- Create a flat single-color version and a transparent-background version. The current paper texture/tonal shading works for a presentation but is less flexible in an application.
- Prepare a simplified globe/A icon for 16–32 px and a separate full emblem/wordmark for larger placements.
- Test navy on ivory, white on navy, grayscale, and 16/24/32 px before committing to final assets.

These are recommendations, not completed logo edits. Final vector artwork should be deliberately drawn and checked rather than presenting a raster tracing as a finished identity. No gladiator illustration or ornamental Greek border is needed to communicate the product.
