# Governance

Aakash Kharb ([@aakash-kharb](https://github.com/aakash-kharb)) maintains AtlasWeave and currently makes final merge and release decisions. Design decisions should be discussed in issues and recorded in architecture decision records when they affect contracts or scope.

Contributors retain attribution and copyright. Additional maintainers may be invited based on sustained, constructive contributions and demonstrated review judgment; access should follow the work they maintain. Major disagreements should be summarized with alternatives and rationale, rather than resolved through repeated unrecorded changes.

This is a solo-maintainer alpha. Release cadence, support windows, and a wider governance model will be revisited as the contributor community grows.
